About Elegy

Elegy is a writing and typesetting app designed for sharing words on visual social platforms.

Write an article, a thought, or a poem. Elegy automatically turns your writing into clean, carefully typeset pages that are ready to save and share.

Choose between Post and Story formats, preview your writing as you type, and let Elegy handle the layout and pagination.

For poetry, Poetry Mode preserves line breaks and spacing, keeping your writing the way you intended it.

No templates to build. No design tools to learn. Just write.
