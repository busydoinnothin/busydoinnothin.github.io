Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Elegy is developed by Busy Doin' Nothin'. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Elegy.

Information Collection

Elegy does not require an account and does not collect personal information from users.

Your Content

Text and other content you create in Elegy are stored locally on your device. Busy Doin' Nothin' does not have access to the content you create in the app.

Cloud Storage

Elegy does not currently use iCloud or another cloud service to sync your writing.

Analytics and Tracking

Elegy does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

Saving and Sharing

Elegy allows you to save images created from your writing. Content leaves the app only when you choose to save or share it using features provided by your device.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Elegy's features or data practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: busydoinnothin.dev@gmail.com
