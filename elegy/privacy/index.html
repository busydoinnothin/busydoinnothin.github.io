Elegy does not require an account and does not collect personal information from users.

Text and other content you create in Elegy are stored on your device. Busy Doin Nothin does not have access to the content you create in the app.

Elegy does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

Sharing

Elegy allows you to save and share images created from your writing. Content is only shared when you choose to save or share it using features provided by your device.
